# Class 11 foundation lesson expansion — 24 September 2026

This is an expansion, not completion of the entire curriculum.

| Subject | New chapter with lessons | New concepts |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Physics | Units and Measurements | 6 |
| Chemistry | Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry | 11 |
| Biology | The Living World | 4 |
| Mathematics | Relations and Functions | 7 |

The existing eight Sets lessons were preserved. There are now 36 published concept lessons across four subjects, out of 463 catalog concepts. Five additional Physics motion lessons remain drafts.

Each new concept contains seven reading sections (goals, explanation, key ideas, rules/notation, worked example, mistakes and summary) and two original checks. These are original authored teaching explanations, not reproduced NCERT text or verified PYQs. Fifty-six corresponding scored-bank records were prepared as drafts; their publication still requires the existing staff review. No reviewer identity or source year was fabricated, and no publication constraint was removed.

Interactive lesson tools cover unit conversion, mass-to-moles, finite function mappings, taxonomy ranks and living/non-living evidence. They use the existing dark palette, accessible controls and reduced-motion support. A numbered task navigation strip opens and saves lesson positions. Route changes clear stale lesson data before fetching the new concept.
